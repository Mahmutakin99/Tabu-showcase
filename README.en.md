# Hadi Anlat!

A Turkish word guessing game for iPhone. Explain the card without using its forbidden words, play against the clock, and make the rules fit your group.

[Türkçe](README.md) · [Screenshots](README.md#ekranlar)

- Solo play and turn based matches for 2–6 teams.
- Classic, reverse and limited word modes.
- Category and difficulty filters, adjustable rounds, swipe or button controls.
- Custom words and categories, statistics, and a local word catalog.
- Themes and coin options in the in-app market.
- SwiftUI with Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, haptics and Reduce Motion support.

The app uses Observation for state, shared gameplay logic, SwiftData for custom cards, and local settings and statistics. Core gameplay uses the bundled catalog; ads and purchases may require connectivity.

This is a public product showcase. Source code remains private, and this repository contains no app binary. No verified store or distribution link is listed yet. Screenshots show a development build and do not establish store availability or testing on every device.
